Chapter 01

Platform Overview

SeeWe AI do cheezein ek hi jagah laata hai: ek job marketplace jahan candidates jobs dhoondhte hain aur recruiters roles post karte hain, aur ek AI interviewer jo shortlisted candidates ke video interviews khud conduct karta hai. Interview ke saare jawab bolke diye jaate hain — AI unhe speech-to-text me convert karta hai, har answer ko evaluate karta hai, aur ek complete rated report banata hai jise recruiter aur client review karte hain.

Platform ka core promise simple hai: hiring with evidence. Traditionally ek recruiter phone screens par ghante bitata hai; yahan AI interviewer 24×7 available hai, har candidate se same structured process follow karta hai, aur har answer ki recording + transcript + score save karta hai. Recruiter ke paas evidence-based decision lene ke liye poora dossier hota hai, aur client ko sirf relevant reports dikhte hain. Candidate ke liye process transparent hai — usse kabhi bhi apna AI score nahi dikhta, sirf clear status updates aate hain.
The hiring pipeline — end to end

Yeh panch-stage pipeline har role ke screen par alag-alag roop me dikhta hai. Candidate ko apne application card par ek animated 5-dot tracker dikhta hai. Recruiter ko Applicants inbox me status badges aur timeline chips dikhte hain. Client ko sirf Stage 4 ke reports dikhte hain jo recruiter ne share kiye hain. Har stage ka data automatically aage flow karta hai — kisi ko manually same data dobara enter nahi karna padta.
Core concepts — jo har role ko pata hone chahiye
🤖 AI Interviewer

Ek virtual interviewer jo candidate ke resume aur job ki JD (job description) dono se milkar questions generate karta hai. Questions bolkar puchhe jaate hain (text-to-speech), candidate bolke jawab deta hai, aur speech engine (ASR) usse text me convert kar deta hai. Total 10 questions hote hain — 6 core skill questions + 4 quick profile questions (Section 3.8 me detail).

🚦 Verdicts — AI ka final recommendation

Interview complete hone par AI overall score (0–10) nikalta hai aur chaar dimensions grade karta hai: Technical Depth, Communication, Problem Solving, Domain Knowledge. Score se verdict banta hai. Verdict sirf recruiter aur client dikhta hai — candidate ko kabhi nahi.

🚀 Strong Hire · 8.0+ ✅ Hire · 6.5+ 🤔 Maybe · 5.0+ ❌ No Hire · below 5.0
🔒 Score privacy — candidate kabhi score nahi dekhta

Yeh platform ka design principle hai, sirf UI rule nahi. Candidate ke interview room, dashboard, notifications aur API responses — sab jagah se AI score, feedback aur verdict server par hi filter ho jaate hain. Candidate ko sirf "Answer submitted" jaise neutral confirmations milte hain. Scores sirf recruiter aur (share hone par) client ke paas jaate hain.

📦 ATS dossier — candidate ka poora record ek payload me

Interview complete hone ke baad recruiter candidate ka complete dossier apne company ke ATS (Applicant Tracking System) me push kar sakta hai — profile, position, interview plan, saare answers, transcripts, scores, report aur client decision. Webhook push ke alawa JSON copy/download aur per-position CSV export bhi available hai (Section 4.6).

3.7 AI Video Interview — step by step (candidate ka sabse important section)
Interview room full-screen experience hai. Usme camera, microphone, aur SeeWe ka AI interviewer hota hai — koi typing nahi. Neeche poora flow diya gaya hai:
1
Room prepare hota hai
"Preparing your interview room…" ke baad AI aapke resume + job ki JD se 10 questions ka plan banata hai. Agar beech me interview adhoora chhoda tha, to wahi se resume hota hai — answered questions waise hi milte hain aur aap pehle unanswered question par land karte hain.
2
Welcome card
"You have been shortlisted 🎉" — aapka naam, "AI video interview · {job title}", aur badges: domain (jaise IT & Software), level (EASY / MEDIUM / HARD), N questions. Checklist aapko batati hai: "Answer out loud — your words are transcribed automatically", "Voice-only room — just speak; no typing needed". Privacy line: "Private room. Nothing is broadcast live — your answers are shared only with the hiring team." "Begin AI Interview ✨" dabane par camera permission maangi jaati hai (mana karne par bhi voice answers chalte rehte hain).
3
Question suniye — AI bolkar puchhta hai
AI interviewer question bol kar puchhta hai (mute button bhi hai). Card par "Question {i} of {N}", focus area badge (jaise "State architecture"), aur Replay button hota hai. Left side par aapka camera preview (mirror view, "You" badge, Rec indicator + mic level bars) aur progress card hai — jisme answered/total count aur tappable question navigator dots hain.
4
Bol kar jawab dijiye
"🎙 Record Answer" dabaiye aur bolna shuru karein. Recording me video + audio dono capture hote hain (camera off ho to sirf audio), 3-minute ki limit hai, aur live REC timer chalta hai. Bolte waqt aapke shabd read-only transcript box me appearance late hain (ASR). Word-count hint bhi milti hai: "{n} words · aim for 40–150 words".
5
Take A / Take B — dobara bolne ka option
Har question par 2 takes record kar sakte hain. "Add Take B" se naya take, preview players se dono compare, "Discard" se bekar take hatao — phir best take submit karo. Jo take discard hota hai wo kabhi upload hi nahi hota; sirf selected take server par jata hai.
6
Submit karein
"Submit Take A ➜" dabane par upload hota hai. AI evaluate karta hai, lekin aapko sirf neutral confirmation dikhta hai — green "Answer received" card: "Your response is safely recorded. The hiring team reviews every answer after the interview." Score, feedback, strengths  — kuch bhi nahi dikhta. "Next Question ➜" se aage badhein.

Score privacy promise

Candidate experience me AI score, verdict, ranking, per-question feedback — kuch bhi nahi dikhta. Ye sirf screen chhupana nahi hai: interview ke API responses bhi server par candidate-safe hote hain (score fields network par travel hi nahi karte). Notification bhi score-free hota hai: "Your interview responses are with the hiring team…". Honest design — aap answers review kar sakte hain, lekin evaluation sirf hiring team ke paas hai.

3.8 Job Alerts — naye jobs ki notification
Alerts tab me "Create a job alert" card hai: Keyword (jaise React, Python, DevOps), Location, optional Min. salary (LPA) — phir "Create alert". Har alert card par: naam (jaise "React in Bengaluru"), "🔔 N new" badge ya "All caught up", total matches, aur 4 sample matching jobs. "Browse matches" se search pre-applied hoti hai. Header ke blue JobAlertsBell se bhi alerts milte hain aur "Manage" se Alerts tab khulta hai. Weekly Email digest preview dialog bhi hai (demo me simulated send hota hai).
3.9 Candidate ke liye pro tips
  • Resume summary sharp rakhein — AI interviewer isi text se questions banata hai; jitna relevant content, utne personalized questions.
  • Profile 100% complete karein — recruiters talent search me poori profile wale candidates ko hi dhoondhte hain (visible toggle ON rakhna mat bhoolen).
  • Interview me 40–150 words per answer bolen — hint bhi yahi deti hai. Ek take kharab lage to Take B record karein.
  • Quiet room, decent lighting — camera optional hai (voice answers bhi count hote hain), lekin video answers hiring team ko zyada confident impression dete hain.
  • Preliminary questions sincerely answer karein — location, experience, notice period aur CTC aapki profile me automatically update hote hain, jisse recruiters ko accurate data milta hai.

4.4 AI interview andar se kaise kaam karta hai
Domain detection: Interview start hone par AI job ke title/skills se domain detect karta hai — 13 buckets: IT & Software, Data & Analytics, Design & Creative, Sales & Business Development, Marketing & Communications, Finance & Accounting, Human Resources, Operations & Support, Education & Training, Healthcare, Legal & Compliance, Hospitality & Travel, General Business. Non-technical domains ke rules me coding questions explicitly ban hote hain — matlab Sales, HR, Finance, Operations sabke liye domain-appropriate questions aate hain. Agar AI kisi reason se questions na bana paye to domain-neutral template fallback chalti hai.
Question plan (10 questions): Q1 intro/experience-fit, Q2–Q5 core skill aur scenario questions (JD ∩ resume overlap se), Q6 practical problem-solving — in sab ke saath rubric points (modelAnswerPoints) hote hain jinke against answers grade hote hain. Q7–Q10 wo 4 preliminary profile questions hain (Section 3.7) jinke spoken answers parse hokar candidate profile me auto-feed ho jaate hain — location, experience, notice period, CTC.
Evaluation: Har core answer ka score 0–10 (1 decimal) hota hai, saath me AI feedback, strengths aur improvements. Final overall score core answers ka mean hota hai aur verdict banta hai (🚀 Strong Hire 8+, ✅ Hire 6.5+, 🤔 Maybe 5+, ❌ No Hire neeche). Report me 4 dimension scores (Technical Depth, Communication, Problem Solving, Domain Knowledge), AI summary, strengths, watch-outs aur recommendation hoti hai. Interview complete hone par aapko notification aata hai: "{verdict emoji} AI interview done · {candidate} · {x.x}/10".
4.5 Interview Hub — sab interviews ek jagah
Hub upar 4 KPI cards dikhata hai: Positions interviewed · Total interviews · Completed · Avg score ({x.x}/10). Ek powerful search box hai — candidate, position, skill, city, verdict label, aur saare question texts me search karta hai. Verdict filter pills: All · ⏳ In progress · 🚀 Strong Hire · ✅ Hire · 🤔 Maybe · ❌ No Hire. Neeche job-wise collapsible groups hain — har group header par interview count, completed count, in-progress count, avg score pill aur "Export CSV" button (18-column ATS-import ready sheet: contact, CTC, notice, score, verdict, timeline sab columns).
Kisi bhi candidate par "Open →" dabane se 360° detail dialog khulta hai. Usme (a) big score tile, (b) 6 auto-captured detail chips — Location, Experience, Notice period, Current CTC, Expected CTC, Headline — jinme interview se capture hui values par teal "via interview" badge hota hai, (c) contact + education + skill-match chips, (d) Journey strip: "Applied {date} · Recruiter viewed {date} · Interview {date}" + application status + client decision, (e) Resume snapshot, (f) "Questions asked & candidate answers" — poora question plan, har question ka focus area, candidate ka transcript, per-question score, AI note, aur inline video/audio player, (g) AI report summary — dimension bars, strengths, watch-outs, recommendation, aur (h) ATS handoff panel.
4.6 ATS Integration — candidate ko apne ATS me bhejna
Interview Hub ke detail dialog me "Send to ATS" panel hai (sirf completed interviews par unlock hota hai): "Push {candidate}'s complete dossier — profile, interview plan, answers, recordings metadata, scores and report — into your own hiring pipeline for the next process."
1
Integration register karein (ek baar)
"+ New integration" → Integration name (jaise "Company hiring pipeline"), Webhook URL (aapke ATS ka POST endpoint, jaise https://ats.yourcompany.com/hooks/seewee), optional API key (x-ats-api-key header me jaati hai) → "Save integration". Integration pause/delete bhi ho sakta hai.
2
Push karein
"Push to ATS" se server aapke webhook par poora dossier JSON POST karta hai (8-second timeout). Success: "{candidate} delivered to {integration}". Fail hone par honest warning dikhti hai aur attempt log ho jata hai — aap phir bhi manual handoff kar sakte hain.

Reference, Privacy & FAQ

Quick reference — statuses, verdicts, notifications, privacy rules, aur aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal.

7.1 Status glossary
StatusMatlab (kise dikhta hai)

7.2 Privacy & data rules
  • Candidate score-blind hai — interview API responses, dashboard, notifications, transcript view: har jagah se score/verdict/feedback server par strip hota hai. Ye defense-in-depth hai, sirf UI hide nahi.
  • Profile visibility toggle — OFF karne par candidate talent search me nahi aata, lekin uski existing applications active rehti hain.
  • Recording retention — default 45 days; recruiter per-client 7–365 days set kar sakta hai. Retention cross hote hi "Clean storage" sweep me recordings delete ho jaati hain (transcript text stay karta hai).
  • Recruiter notes private hain; client ko candidate ke email/phone/resume ka raw text kabhi nahi dikhta.
  • Ek hi decision per report — client verdict immutable hai; dobara decision dene ki koshish par clear error message.
7.3 FAQ
Q: Password kya hai?
Koi password nahi hai — demo-lite sign in hai. Sirf email (aur role) se entry milti hai. Demo chips se ek-click login hota hai.
Q: Candidate ko AI score kyun nahi dikhta?
By design. Score/verdict hiring team ke evidence ke liye hai. Candidate ko sirf process status aur apne answers dikhte hain — isse anxiety kam hoti hai aur evaluation unbiased rehta hai.
Q: Interview beech me chhoda ho jaye to?
Koi problem nahi — interview idempotent hai. Dobara "Resume AI Interview" dabane par wahi se resume hota hai; answered questions safe rehte hain.
Q: Camera nahi hai to interview de sakte hain?
Haan. Camera off hone par bhi voice answers record hote hain — room me "Camera off — your voice answers still count" placeholder dikhta hai. Video answers ka fayda ye hai ki hiring team playback me aapko dekh sakta hai.
Q: ATS webhook fail ho jaye to?
Push attempt honestly log hota hai (✗ + message). Aap tab bhi "Copy JSON" ya "Download" se dossier manual import kar sakte hain, ya per-position CSV use kar sakte hain.
Q: Client decision galat ho gaya, change kaise karein?
Decision locked hai. SeeWe recruiter se contact karein — wo platform team ke through revisit kar sakta hai.
Q: Email digest real me jaata hai?
Demo me digest preview simulated hai — "Send digest" par toast aata hai lekin real email nahi jata. Real email delivery documented next-step hai.
Q: Non-IT jobs ke liye bhi AI interview theek questions puchhega?
Haan — question engine 13 domains detect karta hai (Sales, Finance, HR, Operations, Healthcare, Legal, Hospitality aadi). Non-technical domains me coding questions explicitly blocked hain, aur AI fail hone par domain-neutral fallback questions chalte hain.
Q: Recordings kitne din rehti hain?
Default 45 days. Recruiter apne Clients tab me per-client 7–365 days tak retention set kar sakta hai. Candidate recordings sirf hiring surfaces par play/download hoti hain.

Demo limitations (honest list): passwordless auth (production me real auth chahiye), digest email simulated hai, offline sandbox me external ATS webhook push FAILED log hota hai (manual JSON hamesha kaam karta hai), aur ek client org ke sab users same contact email share karte hain. Ye documented next-round items hain, bugs nahi.
