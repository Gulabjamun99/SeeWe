# MASTER BUILD SPECIFICATION
# COMPLETE JOB PORTAL + RECRUITMENT ATS + AI LIVE AUDIO/VIDEO INTERVIEW PLATFORM

## 1. YOUR ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITY

Act as a senior product architect, product manager, UI/UX designer, full-stack engineer, database architect, AI engineer, real-time communications engineer, security engineer, DevOps engineer and QA lead.

Your task is to design, implement, test and deploy a complete, production-oriented job portal inspired by the functional capabilities of established job platforms such as Naukri.com.

The platform must be an original product. Do not copy proprietary source code, logos, branding, protected designs or copyrighted website content.

This is not a static UI prototype. Build working end-to-end functionality connected to a real database, backend, authentication, file storage, search system, notification system and required integrations.

Do not generate the entire application in one uncontrolled step. First understand the requirements, inspect the existing repository, prepare the architecture and implement the platform in carefully tested phases.

## 2. MOST IMPORTANT PRODUCT DIFFERENTIATOR

The platform must have a built-in AI-based live audio/video interview system.

A recruiter should be able to:

1. Create a job and configure AI interview questions during job creation.
2. Ask AI to suggest ten job-specific interview questions.
3. Edit, delete, reorder, replace and approve the questions.
4. Configure the AI interview opening, closing, language, voice and interview settings.
5. Receive applications or find candidates through the recruiter database.
6. Shortlist a candidate for an AI interview.
7. Send a unique interview invitation link by email.
8. Have the candidate verify their identity using an OTP sent to their email.
9. Let the candidate complete an interactive, AI-led audio/video interview in the browser.
10. Receive an automatic notification when the interview is completed.
11. Review the recording, transcript, answers, question-wise summary and technical status.
12. Decide whether to shortlist, reject, hold or advance the candidate to the next recruitment stage.

The AI interview must be integrated with the job, candidate, application, recruiter, company and ATS records. It must not be an isolated application disconnected from the main portal.

The final hiring decision must remain with an authorized human recruiter or hiring manager.

# PART B — AI LIVE AUDIO/VIDEO INTERVIEW PLATFORM

## 11. DEFINE THE INTERVIEW PRODUCT CORRECTLY

The AI interviewer must conduct a real-time, interactive interview through the candidate's browser.

It must support:

- Live audio conversation.
- Optional live video.
- Spoken AI questions.
- Candidate spoken answers.
- Speech-to-text transcription.
- Question sequencing.
- Answer capture.
- Interview progress tracking.
- Audio/video connection status.
- Interview completion.
- Secure evidence and recording storage, subject to consent and configuration.
- Recruiter review after completion.

Do not implement this as merely a prerecorded video with a list of questions.

Do not implement a fake AI interviewer that displays a question but cannot listen to the candidate's response.

If a real-time AI voice service is required, integrate a suitable provider through a secure backend. If no provider is configured, build the integration interface, setup documentation and honest unavailable state. Never claim a live AI interview works until it has been tested end to end.

## 13. AI-GENERATED QUESTION SUGGESTIONS

When the recruiter enters the job description and required skills, provide a button:

"Generate 10 AI Interview Questions"

Use the job title, job description, required skills, experience level, screening requirements and interview language to generate a relevant set of questions.

The recruiter must be able to:

- Generate ten suggestions.
- Regenerate all suggestions.
- Regenerate one selected question.
- Edit the wording.
- Delete a question.
- Add a custom question.
- Reorder questions.
- Preview spoken wording.
- Mark must-ask questions.
- Approve the final set.
- Save the configuration.
- Reuse a previous approved question set, where authorized.

The AI must not invent job requirements that the recruiter did not specify.

Store the approved question set as a versioned interview template linked to the job.

For an interview invitation already sent, retain the approved question-set version used by that invitation. Editing the job later must not silently modify an already-issued interview.

### Default question structure

Use ten questions as the default, with categories that can be edited:

1. Candidate introduction and current professional role.
2. Relevant experience.
3. Primary required skill.
4. Secondary required skill.
5. Practical example of work performed.
6. Role-specific problem-solving scenario.
7. Relevant project or achievement.
8. Current compensation and compensation expectations.
9. Notice period and earliest joining date.
10. Candidate availability, motivation and closing confirmation.

These are categories, not mandatory identical wording for every job. AI must adapt them to the role.

For example, a Salesforce Business Analyst interview should ask about requirements gathering, Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, stakeholder management and relevant project experience if those skills are required.

A manufacturing maintenance role should ask about plant maintenance, breakdown management, preventive maintenance and other relevant skills only when present in the job requirements.

The AI must not ask irrelevant questions simply to fill ten slots. If a question is not applicable, the recruiter should be able to replace it before approval.

## 17. EMAIL OTP VERIFICATION

When the candidate opens the invitation:

1. Validate the invitation token.
2. Check its expiry and revocation status.
3. Display the job and company details.
4. Ask the candidate to request an OTP.
5. Send the OTP to the intended candidate email address.
6. Let the candidate enter the OTP.
7. Verify the OTP securely on the server.
8. Create a short-lived verified interview session.
9. Allow the candidate to continue to the pre-interview checks.

OTP requirements:

- Cryptographically secure generation.
- Short configurable expiry.
- One-time use.
- Maximum verification attempts.
- Resend cooldown.
- Rate limiting by invitation, account and IP where appropriate.
- Secure storage of OTP hashes.
- Invalidation of previous codes according to defined rules.
- Clear invalid, expired and exhausted-attempt messages.
- Generic errors where necessary to avoid account enumeration.
- Audited verification outcomes without storing the OTP itself in logs.

Do not use email OTP as the only authorization control. The invitation, candidate/job binding, session and interview permissions must also be validated.

Do not require a separate full candidate account merely to attend an interview if a secure guest interview flow is supported. If the candidate already has an account, offer the authenticated flow where appropriate.

## 19. LIVE AI INTERVIEW SESSION

Implement a real-time state machine:

CREATED
→ INVITATION_SENT
→ OTP_VERIFIED
→ PRECHECK
→ CONSENT_CONFIRMED
→ READY
→ IN_PROGRESS
→ PAUSED / RECONNECTING when necessary
→ COMPLETED

Additional terminal states:

- CANCELLED.
- EXPIRED.
- ABANDONED.
- TECHNICAL_FAILURE.
- INVALIDATED.

Define valid transitions and persist them on the server.

### Interview screen

Display:

- AI interviewer status.
- Current question number and total questions.
- Question text.
- AI speaking / listening indicator.
- Candidate microphone status.
- Camera preview when enabled.
- Interview timer.
- Connection status.
- Repeat-question control.
- Audio controls.
- Help and exit controls.
- Clear recording indicator if recording is active.

Keep the interface professional, uncluttered and accessible.

### Question flow

For each approved question:

1. Load the next question from the approved version.
2. Display the question text.
3. Speak the question using the selected Indian voice.
4. Wait until speech playback has completed.
5. Start listening for the candidate's response.
6. Detect speech activity and capture the answer.
7. Convert speech to text using the configured speech recognition service.
8. Store the transcript with timestamps where supported.
9. Detect the end of the answer using a configurable silence threshold and maximum answer duration.
10. Allow the candidate to finish naturally without cutting them off too early.
11. If no response is detected, ask whether the candidate needs the question repeated.
12. Store the answer status and proceed according to the approved rules.
13. Move to the next question.
14. Show progress and remaining questions.

The system must not ask a new question while the candidate is still answering.

Avoid overlapping AI speech and candidate speech.

Support repeating the current question without automatically skipping it.

Where a candidate requests clarification, repeat the approved question or provide only an approved neutral clarification. Do not invent additional job requirements.

### Fixed and dynamic questions

The default interview contains ten recruiter-approved questions.

During a standard interview, the AI must not freely invent new evaluative questions that the recruiter has not approved.

Optional follow-up questions may be supported only if the recruiter explicitly enables them, defines their limits and approves the question-generation policy.

Every generated follow-up must be logged. It must remain relevant to the role and must not request protected or unnecessary sensitive information.

## 20. CTC, EXPECTED CTC, NOTICE PERIOD AND JOINING QUESTIONS

Provide recruiter-configurable compensation and availability questions.

Suggested wording:

"Could you please confirm your current annual compensation, or CTC, and the currency and compensation period?"

"What is your expected annual compensation for this role?"

"What is your current notice period?"

"What is your earliest possible joining date?"

"Are you currently serving your notice period, and if so, when is your expected last working day?"

"Would you be available to work in the location and work arrangement described for this position?"

Important:

- CTC and expected CTC must be separate fields.
- Do not confuse current CTC with expected CTC.
- Store the compensation amount, currency and pay period where applicable.
- If the candidate declines to disclose current compensation, allow that response according to company policy.
- Do not assume zero compensation if the candidate refuses to answer.
- Allow "Not disclosed", "Negotiable" or "Prefer to discuss with recruiter" where appropriate.
- Store notice period as a structured value plus optional explanation.
- Distinguish contractual notice period from actual availability.
- Record the candidate's answer accurately without inventing a value.
- Allow the recruiter to correct a transcription error through an audited correction workflow.
- Do not automatically reject a candidate based solely on these answers.

Questions must remain configurable by role and employer policy.

## 21. SPEECH RECOGNITION AND TRANSCRIPT MANAGEMENT

Implement a speech recognition integration capable of handling the configured interview language.

Requirements:

- Transcribe candidate speech.
- Preserve the original transcript.
- Associate each answer with its question.
- Store timestamps where supported.
- Indicate low-confidence transcription.
- Support correction by an authorized reviewer.
- Preserve original and corrected transcript versions.
- Handle common Indian English pronunciation and technical vocabulary as well as the provider allows.
- Avoid interpreting silence as a negative answer.
- Distinguish transcription failure from a candidate not answering.
- Handle network and service errors gracefully.

Do not silently replace a candidate's spoken answer with an AI-generated interpretation.

- Candidate accidentally closing the tab.
- Microphone disconnection.
- Camera disconnection.
- AI provider outage.
- Speech recognition failure.
- Text-to-speech failure.
- Server timeout.
- Partial recording upload.
- Duplicate browser requests.
- Session expiry.

- Heartbeats and connection monitoring.
- Configurable reconnect grace period.

## 25. INTERVIEW COMPLETION

After the last approved question:

1. Finish the candidate's current response.
2. Save all answers and transcripts.
3. Complete pending media uploads.
4. Verify the interview completion requirements.
5. Mark the interview as completed on the server.
6. Record the completion timestamp.
7. Generate a processing state for transcription or summary tasks still running.
8. Show the candidate a completion confirmation.
9. Send a completion notification to the recruiter.
10. Update the ATS interview status.
11. Queue a post-interview summary job.
12. Notify the recruiter when the review package is ready.

Use distinct statuses such as:

- Interview Completed — Processing.
- Review Ready.
- Review Failed — Retry Available.

Do not confuse completion of the live session with completion of asynchronous recording uploads or AI summarization.

If processing fails, preserve the interview data and allow a retry without asking the candidate to repeat a successfully completed interview.

## 28. AI INTERVIEW SCORING AND FAIRNESS

- Problem-solving evidence.

- Completeness of the answer.

Each assessment must have a clear explanation linked to the candidate's answer or transcript.

Do not use protected characteristics, appearance, facial expressions, accent, perceived personality, gender, age or unrelated personal traits to rank candidates.

Do not invent a numeric score when there is insufficient evidence.

Distinguish these concepts clearly:

- AI summary.
- Evidence-based assessment.
- Technical/transcription confidence.
- Recruiter decision.

The default configuration must not automatically reject candidates.

Recruiters must be able to override an AI assessment, document their reasoning and make the final hiring decision.

Audit changes to scores, rubrics, reviewer decisions and status transitions.

## 29. CANDIDATE POST-INTERVIEW EXPERIENCE

After completion, show:

- Thank-you message.
- Confirmation that the interview was submitted.
- Job title and company.
- A reference ID.
- What happens next.
- Support contact.
- Exit button.

Do not reveal confidential internal notes, recruiter evaluations, other candidates' data or private hiring deliberations.

Do not promise a selection, interview result or response deadline unless configured and approved by the employer.

If the candidate wants to report a technical issue, provide a support link.

## 37. PRIVACY AND CONSENT

- Whether speech will be transcribed.

- Which authorized hiring personnel can access it.

Verify applicable Indian data-protection and other relevant legal obligations before launch.

Do not use candidate media to train unrelated AI models unless a separate lawful basis and appropriate permissions exist.

## 38. FAIR HIRING

Do not make facial emotion analysis, appearance ratings, accent-based ranking or unsupported personality inference part of the hiring system.

Keep interview evaluation tied to job-related evidence.

Make AI-generated summaries explainable and reviewable.

Allow candidates to report technical problems and request reasonable support.

Allow authorized recruiters to correct transcript errors and override automated suggestions.

AI must assist the recruitment process, not make the final hiring decision.

Do not immediately generate hundreds of files without a plan.

- Current database.
- Existing authentication.
- Existing integrations.

Build in this order:

Phase 2: Candidate profile and resume management.

Phase 3: Company, recruiter and job posting.

Phase 4: Job search and applications.

Phase 5: Recruiter candidate database and ATS.

Phase 6: AI question configuration and versioning.

Phase 7: Interview invitation, email OTP and candidate lobby.

Phase 8: Real-time AI audio/video interview.

Phase 9: Recording, transcription, summary and recruiter review.

Phase 10: Admin, verification, support and moderation.

Phase 11: Payments, subscriptions and usage metering.

Phase 12: Analytics, integrations, accessibility and optimization.

Phase 13: Security audit, regression tests and production deployment.

Each phase must have:

- A clear scope.
- Defined dependencies.
- Database changes.
- UI implementation.
- Backend implementation.
- Permissions.
- Validation.
- Error and loading states.
- Automated tests.
- Manual acceptance tests.
- Documentation.
- A completion report.

Do not mark a phase complete when critical tests fail.

Do not replace existing working functionality without inspecting its dependencies and confirming that the replacement works.

If the project is interrupted, preserve progress in a project status document containing completed work, remaining work, current errors, active decisions and exact next steps.

## 42. DEFINITION OF DONE

A feature is complete only when:

- Its UI works.
- Its backend works.
- Its database integration works.
- Permissions are enforced.
- Inputs are validated.
- Errors are handled.
- Loading and empty states are implemented.
- Notifications work where applicable.
- Automated tests pass.
- Integration tests are performed where required.
- Sensitive data is protected.
- Documentation is updated.

For the AI interview feature, the final acceptance test must prove:

1. Recruiter creates a job.
2. AI generates ten relevant question suggestions.
3. Recruiter edits and approves the questions.
4. Candidate is selected from an authorized source.
5. Invitation email is delivered.
6. Candidate verifies email OTP.
7. Candidate completes the pre-interview checks and consent.
8. AI speaks each approved question.
9. Candidate answers using live audio.
10. Video is captured when video mode and recording are enabled.
11. Answers and transcripts are persisted.
12. The interview survives a recoverable interruption.
13. The final answer and recording processing complete.
14. Recruiter receives a completion notification.
15. Recruiter reviews the transcript and available recording.
16. Recruiter chooses the next ATS stage.
17. The candidate status and audit history update correctly.
18. Unauthorized users cannot access the interview or its media.
