SeeWe AI Hiring Portal — Master Flow

Kaun kya karega — Step-by-Step Hiring Flow

Recruiter job post karta hai → Candidate apply karta hai → Recruiter shortlist karta hai → Candidate AI Interview deta hai → Recruiter results dekh kar select karta hai. AI Screening har job ke liye OPTIONAL hai — recruiter ka control, har step pe.

PRIVACY & CONTROL RULES (har phase pe applicable)

  • Candidate ko AI score / verdict / screening dossier KABHI nahi dikhta — sirf recruiter dekhta hai.
  • Mock interview (practice) ka data recruiter ya client ko kabhi nahi jaata — wo sirf candidate ka personal practice hai.
  • AI Screening har job pe OPTIONAL hai — ON/OFF recruiter decide karta hai, per job.
  • Typing (text answer) sirf tab available jab recruiter ne us question ke liye enable kiya ho.
  • AI Interview per job posting skippable ho sakta hai — recruiter ka setting.
  • AI voice = Indian male, slow speed — clear samajh aana zaroori hai.
